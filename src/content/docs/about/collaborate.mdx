In addition to the protocols we provide, a collaboration can include hands-on experience in our lab. For example,
you could be involved in BLink bead preparation and making sample libraries with those beads (including your own
test samples). If you visit for several weeks, you should be well-versed in making your own BLink beads and using
them to make libraries. A visit can also encompass bioinformatic data processing, if time allows and there is interest. 

## How this works
So far, researchers have spent 2-3 weeks as a visiting researcher working with the Genomics Innovation Hub at
Cornell University. During that time, the GIH trains you on making and validating your own batch of BLink beads.
Afterwards, you use your own beads to get the hang of making some sample libraries. In the future, if there is 
sufficient interest, we can move towards a formal training structure with dedicated sign-up workshops. If there
is enough interest, we may also consider formal instruction in linked-read bioinformatics via _Physalia_ or similar.
We are considering a model where we invite multiple independent researchers (2-3) to Cornell at once, which will simplify
accommodation and make the training more efficient. Please reach out if you're interested in that kind of model.
